# Code of Conduct

Every Redfox OSS project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text is the code of conduct for all our repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, email the maintainers at **[debz.exe@gmail.com](mailto:debz.exe@gmail.com)**. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break this code, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
